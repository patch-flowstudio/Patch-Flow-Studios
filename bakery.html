Our story

From a market stall to Mill Street.

“Time does most of the work. We just stay out of its way.”

Hearth & Crumb began as a folding table at the Saturday farmers' market: four kinds of loaf, a cash tin, and a queue that grew a little longer every week. When the stall started selling out before the coffee cart had even opened, we went looking for an oven of our own.

Not much has changed since. Our dough ferments slowly, often overnight, for flavor and a crust that crackles. We bake with flour milled from grain grown nearby, and every loaf, croissant and bun is still shaped by hand on the same wooden bench each morning.
