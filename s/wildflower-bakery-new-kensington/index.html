Before the flowers

A New Kensington landmark, reimagined

Wildflower opened in November 2024 in the space that once held the Bartolacci Brothers tailor shop, a New Kensington landmark run by three Italian brothers who were master tailors. A year later it grew onto Fifth Avenue with Begonia Bistro.
