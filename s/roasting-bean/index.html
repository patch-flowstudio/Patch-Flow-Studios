The story

One of my favorite parts of owning Roasting Bean is finding ways to blend flavors from my Hispanic heritage with the coffee shop experience people know and love.Maribel Curiel, owner, in the N'West Iowa REVIEW, Sep 2026

Roasting Bean opened on August 27, 2021, and celebrates five years in 2026. Beans are sourced from coffee-growing regions around the world, and seasonal drinks, baked goods and events share a piece of Maribel's culture with Hawarden.
