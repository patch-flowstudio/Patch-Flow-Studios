Ordering for a crowd

Customers have ordered pies for a BBQ, holiday trays for Christmas Eve and treats delivered to hospital staff. A simple way to plan yours:

  1. Call with your date, headcount and what you'd like.
  2. Confirm pickup time (or ask about delivery).
  3. Pick up from the porch and enjoy.
