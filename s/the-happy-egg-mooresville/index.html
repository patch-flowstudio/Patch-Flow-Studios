Chef-owned

A longtime dream, near downtown

After decades cooking at well-known Lake Norman and Charlotte-area restaurants, Cornelio Garcia opened The Happy Egg with co-owner Victor DeJesus. The menu is his own homemade recipes, from shrimp and grits with a Cajun cream glaze to Carolina and Chicago hot dogs and a top-selling Reuben.
