Regulars Report: Still the Same Great Cut, Year After Year

A classic barber shop on Broadway where the chairs, the stories and the customers keep coming back.

From the shop floor · sample story

Ask around downtown Edmond and you will hear the same thing: Doc's is a shop that remembers your name. One customer writes that he has been "a devoted customer of this business for over 20 years," and that the wisdom gained just sitting in the shop "is worth any wait."

The menu is simple and done well: haircuts, beard trims and eyebrow trims, with kids welcome in the chair. Another regular, back in Oklahoma since 2020, sums it up as "great cuts, good conversations, long term clientele."

Newcomers find it by word of mouth. "Was looking for a new place to get a haircut and was recommended Doc's Barbershop by a friend," one wrote last spring. "I've found my new place!"
