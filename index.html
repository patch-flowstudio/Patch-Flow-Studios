(06 — Before you ask)

A few
good questions.

How does payment work?+

Half up front once you've seen the sample and want to go ahead, half when you're happy. PayPal or Zelle.

Do I need photos or text?+

Send what you have. We write the copy and can work with photos from your existing listings.

What about hosting and my domain?+

The domain is yours and registered in your name. We help connect it and set up hosting, often free for a one-page site. Domain and hosting costs are separate from the website fee.

Can I ask for changes?+

Of course. Two rounds of revisions are included. Small updates later can be quoted separately.

Do I really own it?+

Yes. Once paid, every file is yours. No monthly charges from us, no retainers, and no lock-in.
