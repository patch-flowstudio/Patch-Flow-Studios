Our place

Potter's Place is a hometown stop in Exeland. Snowmobilers and trail riders know it well: one riding group overflowed the place in 2025 and planned to come back in summer 2026.

It's a cafe that looks after its town too, like the meat raffle held to support a local family after a loss, with thanks to Exeland Fire & Rescue.
