Why "10-39"

Giving back to the people who respond

  • Owned by Brittney and Chance Bailey and Jarett and Audrey Mermis, who serve in police, fire and EMS.
  • Customers can round up their bill, with proceeds going back to local first-responder agencies.
  • A covered park shelter adds overflow seating and room for community events.
  • Their sister stop, 10-39 Treats, serves shaved ice and soft serve in season.
