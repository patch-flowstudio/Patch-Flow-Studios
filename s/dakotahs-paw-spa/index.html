Your dog's spa ticket

How a visit works, so first-timers know what to expect before they call.

  1. Call to book. Tell Dakotah your dog's breed, coat and any nerves.
  2. Drop off for the spa day. Baths, trims and nails, done one dog at a time.
  3. A heads-up if needed. Customers mention Dakotah calls before cutting out matting.
  4. Pick up a fresh, soft pup. Sometimes with a little flower on the collar.
