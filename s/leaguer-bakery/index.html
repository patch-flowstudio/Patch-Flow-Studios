Julie's story

"In this area there's no bakery like ours. We can promote our culture."

Julie Kao grew up next door to a bakery beside her family's pharmacy in Taiwan, and it always smelled wonderful. She later ran a candy factory and took more than 20 years of cooking classes before opening Leaguer Bakery near Wright State in May 2022, baking Taiwanese pastries with less sugar and butter.

Quote and story: Dayton Daily News, Nov 2, 2022.
