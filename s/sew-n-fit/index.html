A tailor shop, built for the work

Sew N Fit is Saeid's tailor shop on 4500 South in Murray. To run it here, he had the building at 192 E 4500 S rezoned for commercial use, and the Murray planning commission recommended it 6–0.

Today the shop handles everything from a missing button to a full custom suit, and customers rate it 4.8 stars across 270 Google reviews.
