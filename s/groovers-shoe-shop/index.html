THE STORY OF THE SIGN

Why it still says Groover's

1940

Groover's Shoe Shop opens in Rome. The DiPrima family purchases the business that same year, and much of the original equipment comes with it.

Sources: RoadsideArchitecture.com; Northwest Georgia News, Oct 2025
The war years

Grandpa Sam Sr. planned to change the metal sign to DiPrima's. Metal was scarce during World War II, so the Groover's sign stayed.

"But during World War II, metal was scarce, so he didn't change it."
Danny DiPrima, Northwest Georgia News, October 2025
Early 1980s

Leo "Danny" DiPrima takes over the shop from his mother after his father, Roman DiPrima, passed away.

Source: Northwest Georgia News, Oct 2025
Today

Walking through Groover's is still like traveling through a time machine: the same address, the same sign, and work done Monday through Friday.

Source: Northwest Georgia News, Oct 2025
