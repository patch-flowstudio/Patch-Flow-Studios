(10) FAQ

Questions owners ask.

How does payment work?

Half up front once you've seen the sample and want to go ahead, half when you're happy. PayPal or Zelle.

How long does it take?

Most one-page sites are ready within a week of getting your details.

Do I need photos or text?

Send what you have. We write the copy and can work with photos from your existing listings.

What about hosting and my domain?

The domain is yours and registered in your name. We help connect it and set up hosting, often free for a one-page site.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes, two rounds of revisions are included. Small updates later can be quoted separately.

Do I really own it?

Yes. Once paid, every file is yours, with no monthly charges.
