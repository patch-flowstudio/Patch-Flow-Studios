How a first visit goes

Parents keep writing about it: a 15 month old's first cut, a son with sensory needs, a ten year old who now asks to go. This is the visit, step by step.

  1. Book or walk in

    Appointments and walk-ins are both welcome. Booking ahead is easiest for little ones.

  2. A chair their size

    A reviewer describes a special chair, a quiet trimmer and a coat just for the littlest clients.

  3. Go at their pace

    Sensory Safe Certified care, with time to settle in before the clippers come out.

  4. Leave with a smile sample

    A finishing touch to confirm with the shop, like the quick braid one guest was surprised with.
