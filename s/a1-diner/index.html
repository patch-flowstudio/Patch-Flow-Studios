A1 Diner

WORCESTER LUNCH CAR #790

A 1946 Worcester lunch car perched on stilts above the Kennebec in historic downtown Gardiner. Classic diner fare plus specials, handwritten tickets and free street parking out front.

The car's story

1946
Built as Worcester Lunch Car #790, born as Heald's.
1947
Opens in Gardiner, on long legs up to street level.
1988
Renamed the A1 Diner; later featured on Food Network's Diners, Drive-ins and Dives.
2018
Longtime employee Aaron Harris takes over as owner and keeps the handwritten tickets going.
